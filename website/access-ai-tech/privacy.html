Access AI policies

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 5, 2026

Access AI is an accessibility-focused application and research project developed by Palmetto Ridge Holdings LLC, operating through Palmetto Ridge Media Group. This policy explains how Access AI handles information on this website, in the Streamlit proof of concept, and in planned native mobile versions.

Privacy questions may be sent to founder@access-ai.tech.

1. Information Access AI may handle

Depending on the features you choose, Access AI may process:

  • Text you submit, including questions, prompts, optional image questions, and conversation content.
  • Images you choose to capture or upload for reading, scene description, object identification, or related visual assistance.
  • Voice recordings you choose to make and transcripts generated from those recordings.
  • Accessibility preferences, such as vision profile, interaction preference, screen-reader or device selection, answer style, and speech preferences.
  • Technical information generated by hosting, browser, or device services, such as IP address, browser or device information, timestamps, and page or feature usage.

The public proof of concept does not currently require a user account.

2. How information is used

Information is used only as needed to provide, improve, secure, and evaluate Access AI, including to answer questions, analyze user-selected images, transcribe user-selected recordings, generate speech, apply accessibility preferences, diagnose failures, and protect the service from misuse.

Access AI is not designed to sell personal information or use user content for targeted advertising.

3. AI, speech, hosting, and analytics providers

The Streamlit proof of concept uses OpenAI API services for AI responses, image analysis, transcription, generated speech, and optional web search. When you use those features, the text, image, audio, search query, or related context required for the request is transmitted to OpenAI. Web-search results may originate from third-party websites.

Fixed onboarding prompts and the Repeat last answer action use the browser's speech-synthesis interface rather than the OpenAI API. Speech may be handled on the device or by a platform speech service, depending on browser, operating system, installed speech engine, and device settings.

The proof of concept is hosted using Streamlit Community Cloud. This public website uses third-party hosting infrastructure.

Analytics status: This replacement website package does not include Google Analytics or another first-party analytics tag. A hosting provider may still generate ordinary security and access logs. If analytics is added later, this policy will be updated before that collection begins.

4. Storage and retention

The Streamlit proof of concept does not intentionally create long-term user profiles or a project-controlled permanent conversation database.

  • Conversation content and accessibility preferences are held in Streamlit session state while the session is active. They are not durable account preferences and can be lost when the session ends or resets.
  • Temporary audio files created for transcription are deleted by the application after the transcription request completes.
  • Camera images are sent for analysis only after the user activates image analysis and are not intentionally written by the application to a permanent local image archive.
  • The separate native Android milestone is planned to store accessibility preferences locally using Android DataStore. Application-level cloud sync is not planned for the initial milestone.

The proof of concept sends Responses API requests with storage disabled. This prevents the application from opting into Responses API application-state storage, but it does not eliminate provider processing or every provider log. OpenAI may retain abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days by default unless approved data controls apply, and exceptional legal or safety retention may apply under the provider's terms.

5. Camera and microphone access

Camera and microphone access are optional and are used only when you choose a feature that needs them. Denying either permission does not prevent use of text-based features.

6. Accessibility-related information

Access AI may process accessibility preferences to personalize how information is presented. These preferences support accessibility functions and are not used for advertising. Avoid submitting unnecessary highly sensitive information when a task can be completed without it.

7. Security

Access AI is designed to use encrypted network connections for production web and API traffic and to keep service credentials out of client-side source code and public repositories. No system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

Access AI is not currently directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13. If a future version is designed for children, required parental or guardian protections will be reviewed before release.

9. Your choices and deletion requests

You may choose not to use camera, microphone, or AI-enabled features. Because the public proof of concept has no user accounts or persistent user-content database, there may be no Access AI account record to delete. Questions about information retained in project-controlled systems may be sent to founder@access-ai.tech.

If later versions add accounts, cloud history, syncing, or persistent profiles, appropriate notices and controls will be provided before those features are released.

10. Safety and sensitive use

Access AI is assistive software, not a guaranteed navigation, medical, emergency, legal, financial, or safety system. AI output may be incomplete or incorrect. Verify high-risk information independently and do not rely on Access AI as the sole source for safety-critical decisions.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may change as Access AI develops. Material changes to data collection, storage, sharing, account behavior, or analytics will be reflected here before the affected feature is broadly released.
